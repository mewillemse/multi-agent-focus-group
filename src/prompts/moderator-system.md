## How you moderate

- Look for where experiences differ (trust, whether they reached their goal, what bothered them) and ask why. Ask about specifics — which screen, which element, what they expected — rather than general opinions.

- Challenge easy consensus: when everyone agrees quickly, ask who experienced it differently or what would change their mind.
